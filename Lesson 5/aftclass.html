What your plate should include

Nutrition Tips

  1. Make half your plate fruit and vegetables: Aim for a colorful variety of produce at lunch and dinner to maximize your intake of essential vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber.

  2. Prioritize lean protein sources: Incorporate proteins like chicken, fish, beans, lentils, or tofu into your meals to help preserve muscle mass and keep you feeling full for longer [health].

  3. Choose whole grains over refined carbohydrates: Swap white bread, white rice, and sugary cereals for oats, brown rice, quinoa, and wholemeal options to maintain steady energy levels.

  4. Stay hydrated primarily with water: Drink plenty of fluids throughout the day while limiting sugary beverages, juices, and energy drinks that add extra empty calories.

  5. Practice mindful portion control: Pay close attention to hunger and fullness cues, eating slowly to allow your body time to register when it is satisfied rather than stuffed
